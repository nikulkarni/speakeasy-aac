SpeakEasy — listen mode

For people who can’t see the screen. SpeakEasy reads each option aloud in a quick, quiet voice. When you hear the one you want, select it by raising your eyebrows, or with any key or switch, and it is spoken out loud: to a person, or to a nearby voice assistant (“Alexa, what time is it?”). Spoken instructions help you get in front of the camera and set up your switch. Everything runs on your device.

Settings

Listening

The cue voice is quicker and quieter than the speaking voice, so the person can tell an option apart from a message meant for the room. Volume differences work in Chrome on a computer; iPhones and iPads ignore them, so pick a different cue voice there.

Switches

Keys: in one-switch setup any key selects. In two-switch setup, Space or an arrow key moves and Enter selects. Escape goes back to the main menu. Most Bluetooth switches send Space or Enter.

Pointer (for sighted users)

Other

Personal words

For a caregiver to fill in once. Names become “Call …” options under People; phrases, places and medicines get their own My words category; and all of them are suggested first on the keyboard. Separate with commas or new lines. Saved only on this device (shared with v5).
