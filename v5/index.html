SpeakEasy — command board

Speak whole commands hands-free. Pick a category, then a command, and the app says it out loud — to a person, or to a nearby voice assistant (“Alexa, turn on the air conditioner”). Select by raising your eyebrows (or pressing Space) as the highlight passes each row and tile — or turn off Scanning in Settings to point with your head and hold still on a tile. Need something that isn’t on a tile? Choose Type a message — it suggests the person’s own names, medicines, and phrases (set them up in Settings). Everything runs on your device.

Default: scanning — raise your eyebrows to select. To change how you select, open Settings at the top right.
