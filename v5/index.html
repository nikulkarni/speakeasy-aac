SpeakEasy — command board

Speak whole commands hands-free. Pick a category, then a command, and the app says it out loud — to a person, or to a nearby voice assistant (“Alexa, turn on the air conditioner”). Select by holding your head still on a tile, or turn on Scanning in Settings to use a single eyebrow raise, blink, or the Space key. Everything runs on your device.
